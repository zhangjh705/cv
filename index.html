About Me

I'm a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at The University of Hong Kong, working in the Electrical and Electronic Engineering department. My research focuses on magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), including compressed sensing, parallel imaging, low-rank reconstruction, abdominal and cardiac dynamic reconstruction, interventional MRI reconstruction, and image super-resolution.

I hold a Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from The University of Hong Kong, an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Columbia University, and a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Xi'an Jiaotong University. I'm passionate about applying deep learning and AI to solve challenges in medical imaging and healthcare.
